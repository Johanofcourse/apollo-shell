Apollo Dashboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-11.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address and basic profile information. That's the only personal data this app ever collects, and it's used for exactly one thing: confirming your identity and checking your email against a private access list before letting you into the dashboard.

What we don't do

We never see or store your Google password. We don't access your Gmail, Drive, calendar, or any other Google data - only the email address itself. We don't sell, share, or use your information for advertising, and we don't hand it to any third party.

How long we keep it

Your email is held in a server-side session for as long as you're signed in, and separately in a short internal list of email addresses authorized to access this dashboard. Signing out clears your session immediately.

Questions

This is a small, independently-run project, not a company. Reach out via the contact info listed on the Google Cloud project tied to this app if you have questions about your data.
