What is this page?

Four real comparisons across the utilities this project tracks, built from the same closed-event records every other page here reads — nothing modeled or estimated. Coverage varies by utility (some report every individual incident; others only ever give one county-wide rolling total), so a smaller or newer utility having a short bar below usually means less historical data, not a better track record. Where a number is thin enough to not mean much yet, it's labeled limited rather than hidden.
